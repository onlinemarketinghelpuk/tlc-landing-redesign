Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

All charters depart from Butler's Wharf Pier, Shad Thames, London SE1 2YE. It's a short walk from London Bridge and Tower Hill stations, and moments from the City and Canary Wharf.
Every charter includes exclusive hire of the vessel, a dedicated event manager, an experienced in-house crew, and on-board catering from FoodbyDish with matched fine wines. Entertainment upgrades (DJ, live music, photo booth) and the Tower Bridge lift are available as add-ons.
We recommend booking at least 2 to 3 months ahead for most events. For December and peak summer dates, 4 to 6 months is advisable. You can secure your date with just a 25% deposit.
Absolutely. Your event manager will work with your suppliers to coordinate everything, from florists to live bands. We also have our own in-house entertainment packages if you'd prefer us to handle it.
All three vessels have heated, enclosed interiors with full event facilities, so your party goes ahead regardless of the weather. The Elizabethan and Edwardian also have covered and open deck areas, giving guests the option to step outside when conditions allow.
The Dixie Queen suits large celebrations (up to 620 guests), the Elizabethan is ideal for weddings and mid-sized parties (up to 235), and the Edwardian is perfect for intimate dinners and exclusive gatherings (up to 90). Send us an enquiry and your event manager will recommend the best fit.
